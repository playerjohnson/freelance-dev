Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Data Controller

This website is operated by Anthony D Johnson, trading as a freelance software developer, based in Peterborough, United Kingdom. For data protection enquiries, contact: johnsons496@googlemail.com.

What Data I Collect & Why

This site collects minimal personal data. Here is what I collect and the lawful basis for each:

Contact enquiries (name, email address, service interest, budget range, and message): sent through Formspree when you submit the contact form, or received by email when you contact me directly. Lawful basis: legitimate interest — to respond to your business enquiry and discuss the services you've requested. Formspree processes form submissions on my behalf; see its privacy policy.

Optional analytics and advertising are temporarily paused. This freelance website does not load Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager or Google AdSense while the pause is in place. The contact form remains available.

Cookies

Optional analytics and advertising remain off for both new visits and visits with previously saved choices. There are no controls to enable them during this pause.

This website does not read, save or change optional-cookie preferences during the pause. Preferences and cookies used by other parts of this domain are left untouched.

The pause does not erase data already sent to providers or cookies already stored. Reload any page opened before the pause to stop its previously loaded optional scripts.

Third-Party Services & Data Processors

I use the following third-party services that may process your data:

Formspree Inc. (United States): processes contact form submissions on my behalf. Formspree stores submission data on servers in the United States. See their privacy policy.

Google LLC (United States): supplies the web fonts used by this website and the email service used to receive and reply to enquiries. Formspree forwards enquiry notifications to this mailbox. Optional Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Google AdSense services are currently paused. See Google's privacy policy.

GitHub Inc. (United States): hosts this website on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical logs including IP addresses as part of their hosting infrastructure. See GitHub's privacy statement.

International Data Transfers

Using these services involves processing outside the UK. Formspree states that it hosts its services in the United States and relies on standard contractual clauses as a processor; see its security and privacy information. Google and GitHub describe their international processing and transfer arrangements in the privacy notices linked above. Contact me at johnsons496@googlemail.com to ask about the safeguards applicable to your enquiry and how to obtain a copy.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for up to 12 months to allow for follow-up communication, after which they are deleted. The tracking pause does not delete data previously sent to analytics providers. Previously saved cookie preferences may remain in your browser, but this website does not read or change them during the pause.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of any personal data I hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to restrict processing — request that I limit how I use your data.
  • Right to data portability — request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to withdraw consent — contact me using the email below about any processing based on your consent. Optional analytics and advertising are currently paused on this website.

To exercise any of these rights, email johnsons496@googlemail.com. I will respond within one month as required by UK GDPR.

Complaints

If you believe your data protection rights have not been respected, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):

  • Website: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
  • Helpline: 0303 123 1113

I would appreciate the opportunity to address your concerns before you contact the ICO, so please reach out to me first at the email above.

Children's Privacy

This website is not intended for use by anyone under 18 years of age. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data through the contact form, please email me and I will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top indicates the most recent revision. Material changes will be highlighted on the site.
